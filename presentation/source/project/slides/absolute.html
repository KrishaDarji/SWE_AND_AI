Median new LOC per ladder step, averaged over the three backgrounds (9 runs each). Steps 6–10 cost 0.84–0.99× steps 1–5 for the LLM + reuse arms, and 0.43× for the human team.

Shrinking code at scale

~1:15. This is the honest twist. Reuse makes LLM steps cheaper, about 65 lines instead of about 105. But the cost per step doesn’t keep shrinking: step 10 costs about the same as step 2. Only the human team shows a real downward curve, from 198 lines to 36. So for LLMs we support reuse, but not shrinking.
