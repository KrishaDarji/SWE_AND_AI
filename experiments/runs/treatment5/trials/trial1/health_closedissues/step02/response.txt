Implemented the independent Python standard-library solution in source/guess_model.py with tests in tests/test_guess_model.py.

It predicts MRE- and ACC+ from exactly the five requested settings, normalizes numeric predictors from training rows only, one-hot encodes the training criterion vocabulary, treats missing numeric values as the training mean, missing criterion as an explicit missing value, and unseen criteria as all-zero categorical indicators. Identical normalized configurations are grouped before a reproducible seed-42 80/20 split. The fit is a small ridge-stabilized linear estimator solved with Gaussian elimination; predictions and held-out MAE are separate from the training-mean baseline.

EZR machinery adapted: seeded shuffle/holdout discipline from ezr-lib, NUM-style running mean and standard deviation, SYM-style categorical vocabulary/count handling, and the separation of training summaries from evaluation rows. No Lua is executed.

Results: 4 tests passed. Demonstration: 8,000 training rows and 2,000 held-out rows; MRE- held-out MAE 0.09386913284820107 versus baseline 0.16604018269382761; ACC+ held-out MAE 0.05213793957326888 versus baseline 0.09007118811959755; measured real time 0.08 seconds.

Assumptions and limitations: rows with invalid targets are excluded; valid zero targets remain valid; fewer than two groups or empty partitions raise ValueError; configuration fields must be exact; the linear model is a simple prediction baseline and does not claim a domain metric formula, valid range, unit, or compute-cost interpretation.
