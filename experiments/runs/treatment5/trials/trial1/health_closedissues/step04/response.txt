Implemented the independent Python flow skill in the exact Step 4 directory.

Files include:
- source/flow.py: streaming CSV iterator, Welford running mean/sample variance, per-outcome valid counts, directional best values, complete configurations, record IDs, deterministic first-seen tie handling, and defensive snapshots.
- tests/test_flow.py: 4 tests covering batch agreement, all outcome directions, ties, empty/one-row input, missing/nonfinite values, iterator consumption, CSV integration, and copying.
- test_output.txt and runtime_output.txt: preserved final test and demonstration logs; attempt-labeled logs are retained too.
- metrics.csv, run_instructions.txt, prompt.txt, and snapshots.jsonl.

EZR machinery adapted: ezr-lib's iterator/CSV streaming ideas, ezr.lua's NUM.add one-pass summary (translated as Welford updates), and the library's least/min-so-far reducer pattern. No Lua is executed and no packages are used.

Assumptions and limitations: record IDs are one-based CSV arrival positions; ties retain the earliest record; missing, unparsable, NaN, and infinite outcomes are excluded independently; configurations are retained as strings from CSV; CSV order is only a demonstration sequence, not a verified timeline. Running summaries are not predictions for unseen configurations. This implementation makes no convergence, evidence sufficiency, cost, or automatic stopping claim.
