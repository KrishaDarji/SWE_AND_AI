Implemented the Python streaming evaluator in source/flow.py, with tests in tests/test_flow.py and a demonstration runner in run_demo.py.

The implementation processes CSV records incrementally, maintains separate valid counts, means, sample variances, and directional best values for MRE- (minimize), ACC+ and PRED40+ (maximize). It retains the five configuration fields and record identifier, keeps earliest ties, ignores missing/nonfinite outcomes, and defensively copies configurations and snapshots. Empty input and one-observation variance are defined.

EZR machinery adapted: NUM:add became Welford’s one-pass update, NUM:div became sample variance calculation, iter became generator-based CSV consumption, and stable earliest-winner behavior follows EZR’s deterministic ordering idea. No Lua is used.
