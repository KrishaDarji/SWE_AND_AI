Approach:
Read the CSV with csv.DictReader, select the last finite numeric column as the
benchmark measurement, and compute its median and median absolute deviation
(MAD). Report rows whose modified z-score has absolute value at least 3.5,
including the row number, value, score, and configuration fields.

Assumptions and limitations:
- The last finite numeric CSV column is the response measurement.
- Rows are independent enough for a robust global screen.
- A MAD of zero treats values different from the median as infinitely unusual.
- This detects marginal outliers; it does not model interactions among encoder
  settings, repeated-run variance, or causal measurement error.
- The detector does not infer whether an anomaly is high or low quality beyond
  the sign of the score.
