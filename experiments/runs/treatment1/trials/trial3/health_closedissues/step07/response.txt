Approach: score each row as 0.4*ACC+ + 0.4*PRED40+ + 0.2*(1-MRE-) and select the maximum. Ties prefer lower MRE-, higher accuracy, higher PRED40+, then the earlier row.

Assumptions and limitations: metrics are numeric and bounded [0,1]; ACC+ and PRED40+ are higher-is-better and MRE- is lower-is-better. Weights encode the intended balance and may need domain validation. This selects an evaluated row; it does not retrain or independently validate a model.
