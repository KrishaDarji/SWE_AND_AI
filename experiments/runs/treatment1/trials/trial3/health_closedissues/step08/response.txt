Approach:
Load the CSV with csv.DictReader, parse the five hyperparameters, use the first data row as the current configuration, and keep observed rows with a lower MRE-. Rank candidates by the number of changed hyperparameters, then lower MRE-, then row number. The measured recommendation changes N_estimators 40 to 10 and Min_impurity_decrease 2.75 to 5.0, improving MRE by 0.0475699558174.

Assumptions and limitations:
- The first data row is the baseline configuration.
- MRE- is the error metric and lower is better.
- Only configurations already present in the dataset are considered.
- “Smallest” means fewest changed hyperparameter fields; no model is retrained.
- The result is observational and does not establish causal improvement.
