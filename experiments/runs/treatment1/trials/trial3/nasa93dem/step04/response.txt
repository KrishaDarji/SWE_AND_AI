Approach: load historical projects with csv.DictReader, rank each arriving proposal by a mixed numeric/categorical distance, estimate four outcomes from the nearest five projects, and emit confidence plus risk flags as JSON. Proposals with known outcomes can be added to the stream context.

Assumptions and limitations: proposal fields use the dataset's names and categorical values; numeric distance is scaled by a fixed 100-unit factor; nearest-neighbor estimates are simple averages, not calibrated forecasts; missing fields are treated as mismatches; no external packages or persistence are used.
