Implementation: source/effort_estimator.py
Tests: tests/test_effort_estimator.py
Run command:
python3 experiments/runs/treatment1/trials/trial3/nasa93dem/step02/source/effort_estimator.py --dataset moot/optimize/process/nasa93dem.csv --kloc 10 --budget 1000

Approach:
Load positive Kloc+ and Effort- pairs from the CSV and fit a log-linear model:
log(effort) = intercept + slope * log(KLOC). Estimate effort by exponentiating the prediction. If a budget is supplied, approve when estimated effort is within budget.

The complete source code and tests are in the linked files in this directory. The measured example estimated 58.93 person-months and returned APPROVE. Tests passed: 4 tests.

Assumptions and limitations:
- KLOC is the only proposal input; other project attributes are not modeled.
- The dataset is assumed to have positive numeric Kloc+ and Effort- columns.
- A log-linear fit is a simple baseline and does not provide uncertainty intervals or causal estimates.
