Approach:
The program validates and reads the CSV using the standard library. It measures Pearson association between each numeric hyperparameter and MRE-/ACC+, compares each numeric setting across the median high-error/low-error splits, and reports per-level means and counts for criterion. Findings are ranked by a combined directional association score. The output explains that positive MRE association and negative ACC association indicate worse observed outcomes.

Observed result on 10,000 rows: Min_sample_leaves is the strongest signal (MRE r=0.803, ACC r=-0.799); higher values coincide with higher error and lower accuracy. Among criteria, absolute_error has the highest mean MRE (0.751) and lowest mean ACC (0.478), followed by poisson and squared_error.

Assumptions and limitations:
- MRE- is treated as prediction error, and ACC+ as accuracy; larger MRE and smaller ACC are worse.
- All required columns are present and numeric target/setting values are finite.
- Correlations and group means describe this sampled configuration table; they do not establish causation, account for interactions, or replace controlled ablation/validation.
- The median split is a readable diagnostic, not a model of the response surface.
