Approach:
Read historical x264 configurations, normalize each setting's range, select the nearest rows, and calculate an inverse-distance weighted mean of Performance-. Return neighbor spread as a simple uncertainty indicator.

Assumptions and limitations:
- Performance- is treated as encoding time.
- The proposed configuration contains every non-measure CSV field.
- Numeric values are compared by normalized distance; other values by equality.
- Nearest-neighbor prediction is descriptive and may be inaccurate for configurations unlike the historical data.
