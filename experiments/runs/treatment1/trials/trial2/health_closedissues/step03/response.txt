Approach: treat the `criterion` value as the configuration family. Normalize
MRE in the lower-is-better direction and ACC/PRED40 in the higher-is-better
direction, average the three equally weighted normalized metrics, and compare
family means. A deterministic independent bootstrap estimates a 95% confidence
interval for each pairwise mean-score difference. A family is called reliable
when that interval excludes zero.

Assumptions and limitations: the CSV's criterion field defines the family;
metrics are equally weighted; min-max normalization uses the complete dataset;
rows are treated as independent; bootstrap results are approximate and use a
fixed seed; this is observational evidence, not a causal experiment.
