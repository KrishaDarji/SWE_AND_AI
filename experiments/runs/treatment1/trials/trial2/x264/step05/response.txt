Approach: read the CSV with csv.DictReader, treat Performance- as encode runtime, define slow encodes as the top quartile by runtime, and compare each binary setting's enabled and disabled groups. Rank settings using standardized mean-runtime difference plus slow-rate lift, while also reporting point-biserial correlation.

Assumptions and limitations: binary columns are encoded as 0/1; larger Performance- means slower; the top 25% is a useful operational definition of slow; observational associations do not establish causation; interactions among settings are not modeled; missing or malformed values are not supported.
