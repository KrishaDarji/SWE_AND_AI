Approach: read the CSV, normalize Effort-, Defects-, and MonthsX by Kloc+, take
log ratios, and rank records by robust median/MAD deviation. The measured top
candidate is project id 89 (score 5.309; mean score 2.596), whose scale-adjusted
outcomes are unusually high relative to the historical records.

Assumptions and limitations: Kloc+ and all three outcomes are numeric and
positive; robust univariate scores do not prove that a record is erroneous;
relationships among categorical project attributes are not modeled; the result
is sensitive to the selected outcome fields and the small historical sample.
