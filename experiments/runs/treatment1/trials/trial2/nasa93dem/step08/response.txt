Approach: estimate risk as mean defect density (Defects-/Kloc+). For each categorical process driver, compare adjacent rating levels, estimate the risk reduction from observed defect densities, assign one cost unit per rating step, and select the lowest-cost positive reduction. The implementation uses only the Python standard library.

Measured result: changing ltex from l to vl was selected, with estimated risk reduction 26.190176440882126 and cost 2 units. Tests passed: 2.

Assumptions and limitations: rating order is the conventional vl < l < n < h < vh < xh order; adjacent observed groups are treated as comparable; historical associations are not causal estimates; cost units are a relative proxy rather than monetary costs; omitted or confounded project factors can change the recommendation.
