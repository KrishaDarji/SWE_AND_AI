Approach: the estimator is deterministic 5-nearest-neighbor regression. Numeric predictors are standardized by training-set population standard deviation; categorical predictors use mismatch distance. The historical baseline predicts query KLOC multiplied by the training-only median effort/KLOC. Both use identical leave-one-out evaluation over all 93 rows and report effort MAE.

The baseline is historical median productivity and can be affected by small or unrepresentative training samples.
