Implemented the reusable Python standard-library summary application and tests in the exact requested directory.

Source: source/x264_summary.py
Tests: tests/test_x264_summary.py

The implementation loads CSV records, derives setting columns and the `Performance-` outcome from the header, reports record count, setting frequencies, missing/invalid outcomes, and numeric count/min/max/mean/median/stdev. It explicitly handles empty input, header-only input, absent outcomes, missing values, and invalid/non-finite outcome values. The real dataset run found 1,152 records, 1,152 valid outcomes, no missing or invalid outcomes, mean 461.754, median 452.779, minimum 244.23, and maximum 821.963; lower Performance- is better.

EZR machinery adapted: `ezr-lib.lua`'s CSV row streaming idea became Python's `csv.reader`; EZR's header-driven column roles and trailing `-` minimization convention informed outcome identification; EZR's numeric column summaries informed the descriptive statistics. No Lua dependency or third-party package is used.

Tests: 4 passed. Run commands and captured logs are in run_instructions.txt, test_output.txt, and runtime_output.txt. metrics.csv records the measured demonstration time and preserved repaired command failures.

Assumptions and limitations: the first CSV row is the header; the first header ending in `-` is the outcome; all other columns are settings; CSV quoting follows Python's standard parser; missing tokens are empty, `?`, `nil`, `NA`, `N/A`, and `null`; malformed numeric outcomes are counted invalid rather than inferred; rows are padded/truncated to the header width; no units or additional quality measures are invented.
