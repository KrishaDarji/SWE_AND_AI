The implementation loads CSV projects while preserving 1-based physical CSV positions and idX values. It reports 93 records, all 28 columns, 22 categorical descriptor frequency tables, metadata roles (idX, centerX, YearX, MonthsX), duplicate idX {'29': 2}, and numeric summaries for Kloc+, Effort-, and Defects-. Kloc+ is labeled maximize and the two cost outcomes minimize only as the dataset goal convention; this does not claim larger projects are inherently better.

Missing tokens are blank, ?, NA, N/A, null, and None. Blank rows are skipped; malformed rows are reported and skipped. Numeric missing, invalid, and nonfinite values are distinguished. Categorical values are counted as strings and never assigned ordinal distances. Empty and header-only inputs are supported.

EZR machinery adapted: CSV streaming, symbolic frequency counts, one-pass/Welford numeric accumulation, and sample standard deviation (n-1 denominator). No Lua or third-party package was used.
