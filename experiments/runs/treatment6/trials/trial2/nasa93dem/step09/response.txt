4. Limitations
This is marginal historical effort screening. It does not adjust for project size or other descriptors. Both unusually low and high effort can be flagged. A flag warrants investigation but does not prove a measurement error or establish a cause. Zero MAD and insufficient leave-one-out references are intentionally undefined.
