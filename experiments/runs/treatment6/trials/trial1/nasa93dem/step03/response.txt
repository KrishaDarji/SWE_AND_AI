The certification compares observed Effort- for tool=n (A) versus tool=h (B), strips whitespace, reports counts, means, A-minus-B difference, a Welch–Satterthwaite 95% interval, and a two-sided Welch test. It separately reports statistical evidence, point-estimate threshold comparison, and whether the complete interval exceeds a practical threshold. The demonstration threshold is illustrative: 10% of the tool=n mean.

The interval crosses zero and practical thresholds, so this does not establish a reliable difference or equivalence.

EZR background informed reuse of the existing descriptive/group machinery and its random/statistical comparison conventions. The new numerical work uses standard-library math: an incomplete-beta continued fraction, binary-search Student-t quantiles, and Welch–Satterthwaite arithmetic; the t quantile is checked against t(.975,10)=2.22813885. No packages or Lua runtime are used.

Limitations: Effort- is observed effort, not verified monetary cost. Skew, imbalanced groups, missing/nonfinite values, zero variance, and project-scope differences can affect interpretation. This is observational and does not claim causal process effects, repeated-run noise, invented units, or guaranteed savings. A non-significant result is not equivalence; a threshold-crossing interval leaves practical importance uncertain.
