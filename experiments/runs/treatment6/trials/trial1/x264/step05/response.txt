Blame ranks each encoder setting by eta-squared: between-setting sum of squares divided by total outcome sum of squares. Lower Performance- is better. Each setting reports score, rank, valid/missing counts, level count, and per-level count/mean/median/min/max. Constant and single-level settings receive zero; missing/nonfinite cells are excluded per setting; categorical levels are supported; ties are deterministic by setting name. Results are marginal observational associations, not causal responsibility. Correlated or mutually exclusive settings can share explanatory information, so a low score does not prove irrelevance.

EZR machinery adapted from the authorized ezr.lua and ezr-lib.lua includes CSV-row streaming, finite-value handling, incremental summary statistics, sorted deterministic keys, and variance/diversity-based group comparison. No Lua runtime or third-party package is used.
