The chooser builds a pool of complete valid binary configurations, normalizes numeric binary spellings, shuffles candidates with local seed 42, and reveals outcomes only through evaluations. Budget counts attempted oracle calls, including missing or invalid outcomes. Duplicate configurations share one candidate and use the first original position, even when that outcome is invalid. Winners are selected by lowest finite Performance-, then earliest original position.

Added choose_rows and choose, CLI flags --choose, --budget, and --seed, explicit handling for invalid/zero/oversized budgets, empty pools, and no successful evaluations, evaluation history, complete settings, positions, and usage counts. Added a separate retrospective historical replay audit that cannot affect the winner and is explicitly not newly measured encoding or a future-performance guarantee.

The EZR reference informed the streaming/lower-is-better summary style, deterministic ordering, and explicit missing/invalid accounting. No Lua or third-party package was used.
