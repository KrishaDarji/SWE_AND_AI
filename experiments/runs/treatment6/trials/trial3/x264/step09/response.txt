Implemented Step 9 spot detection in source/summary.py and preserved the Step 8 source, tests, APIs, and demonstrations.

The new spot_rows/spot functions normalize complete binary settings, use Hamming distance <= 1, count reference rows (including duplicates), exclude the observation itself, require at least 3 reference rows, and compute median plus MAD around that median. Scores are absolute deviation/MAD with threshold 3.5. A zero MAD produces an undefined score unless deviation is zero. Missing/nonfinite outcomes and invalid/nonbinary settings are excluded with original one-based positions; rows without adequate reference data are reported separately. Both unusually high and low results may flag, and a flag warrants investigation rather than proving an error.

The authorized Step 8 implementation was copied into this snapshot and extended without imports from earlier directories. The EZR references informed the compact CSV/row normalization and descriptive-statistics style; no Lua or third-party package was run.

Verification: 38 tests pass. The real X264 dataset demonstration completed successfully: 1152 eligible rows, 0 exclusions, 0 flags, real time 0.73 seconds. Preserved demonstrations were also run and their direct captures are saved in this directory. The first failed test attempt and repaired attempts remain separately labeled.

Known limitations: Hamming radius, minimum reference size, and threshold are fixed defaults for this step; local references can be sparse; duplicate rows are not collapsed; undefined zero-scale scores are not flags; and results depend on the supplied measurements and binary-field interpretation.
