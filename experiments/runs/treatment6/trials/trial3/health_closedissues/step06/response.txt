The implementation treats this as a retrospective recommendation from observed complete rows, not a prediction for an unevaluated configuration. It selects lowest MRE-, then highest ACC+, then highest PRED40+, then earliest original CSV position. Each historical median is independently computed from all finite values for that outcome. Missing/nonfinite settings make a row ineligible; missing/nonfinite outcomes also make it ineligible for recommendation. Blank rows are ignored, malformed rows are excluded without renumbering, and original physical CSV positions are retained.

EZR informed the descriptive-summary and marginal-association style: explicit finite/missing conventions, simple robust summaries, and association results labeled as global historical context rather than causal or row-level effects. No units, metric ranges, runtime claims, resource costs, prediction guarantees, or compute budget were introduced.
