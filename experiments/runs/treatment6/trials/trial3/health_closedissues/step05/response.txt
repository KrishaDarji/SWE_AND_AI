Reused the complete Step 4 source and tests, adding `blame()` and `run_blame.py`. The analysis ranks exactly N_estimators, criterion, Min_sample_leaves, Min_impurity_decrease, and Max_depth against MRE- using marginal eta-squared (between-group SS / total SS). Criterion uses exact categorical groups; numeric settings use up to four equal-frequency bins with midpoint boundaries determined before outcome inspection. Results include eligible/excluded counts, group counts, means, boundaries, sums of squares, directional group means, and deterministic tie handling. Undefined scores are reported for empty, insufficient, constant, or single-group data.

The EZR reference informed the conservative parsing, missing-value handling, summaries, and explicit lower-is-better treatment, but no Lua was run. Associations are marginal historical associations only; they do not establish causation, significance, independent importance, or guaranteed improvement.
