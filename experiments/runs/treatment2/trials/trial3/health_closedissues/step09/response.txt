in source/main.py and source/model_summary.py.

Reuse: Step 8 source and tests from this exact Treatment 2 Trial 3 Dataset 3

Assumptions and limitations: quality columns are inferred from MRE/ACC/PRED
prefixes unless explicitly supplied; robust outlier flags are screening signals,
not proof of measurement error; duplicate disagreement only indicates that a
configuration was observed more than once with differing quality values; all
processing uses Python standard-library modules only.
