Implemented Step 2 by preserving Step 1's summarize_csv API and CLI, then adding predict_quality and --predict-config. The predictor uses range-normalized numeric distance, exact categorical matching, and a distance-weighted average of nearby historical rows. Tests cover all Step 1 behaviors plus prediction and its CLI. See runtime_output.txt and test_output.txt for measured runs.

Assumptions and limitations: MRE- is treated as model error and ACC+ as accuracy; the CSV contains historical evaluated configurations; omitted configuration fields are wildcards; this is a heuristic forecast and cannot establish generalization or replace full evaluation. Only Python standard-library modules are used.
