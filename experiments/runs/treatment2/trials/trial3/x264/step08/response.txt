Implemented Step 8 by reusing the complete Step 7 source and tests, then adding
suggest_setting_change() and the suggest.py command-line wrapper. The suggestion
examines observed configurations at Hamming distance one from a supplied baseline
and selects the best mean objective among them.

Tests: 20 passed, 0 failed.
Dataset run: for the slowest observed baseline, changing no_mixed_refs from 0 to 1
changed Performance- from 821.963 to 671.400, an improvement of 150.563.

Run command:
python3 -m unittest discover -s experiments/runs/treatment2/trials/trial3/x264/step08/tests -t experiments/runs/treatment2/trials/trial3/x264/step08/tests

Assumptions and limitations: the baseline must be fully specified and observed;
only observed one-setting changes are considered; means are unweighted; the
default objective is the final CSV column and is treated as lower-is-better;
the result is observational and does not establish causality.
