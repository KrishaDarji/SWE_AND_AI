Reusable design:
The core separates loading/summarization from presentation. summarize_csv accepts an optional sequence of measure columns, defaults to the final CSV column, and returns immutable dataclasses. New skills can reuse the data model and renderer or add alternate renderers without changing parsing.

Known assumptions and limitations:
- The CSV has a header and each selected measure is numeric after whitespace trimming.
- Columns not selected as measures are treated as categorical encoder settings.
- Empty datasets and missing values are not given special imputation behavior; numeric failures raise ValueError.
- The current summary reports distributions and aggregate statistics, not correlations or per-setting quality effects.
