On the real dataset, `ref_9` is the strongest slow-tail association: mean penalty 276.72 time units and slow-tail enrichment 0.667. `no_fast_pskip` is next by tail enrichment (0.293) and has a 57.02 mean penalty; `no_mbtree` follows (0.121 enrichment, 50.74 penalty). Known limitations: the analysis treats Performance- as lower-is-better encoding time; it only analyzes binary 0/1 settings; it uses marginal comparisons and does not control for interactions or confounding; the slow tail is defined by a configurable quantile; and all CSV rows are loaded into memory.
