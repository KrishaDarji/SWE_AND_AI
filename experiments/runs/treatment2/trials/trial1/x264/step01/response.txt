The reusable API is summarize_csv(path). It detects binary numeric columns as encoder settings and other numeric columns as quality measures, while retaining statistics and a JSON-compatible result for later skills to extend.

Assumptions and limitations: the CSV has a header; blank or non-finite values are not supported as numeric observations; binary settings are represented as exactly 0/1; all rows are loaded in memory; the dataset's `Performance-` field is treated as a quality measure without inferring whether higher or lower is better.
