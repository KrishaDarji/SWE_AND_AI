Assumptions and limitations: Performance- is treated as a larger-is-slower measurement because the dataset's observed values are ranked directly. The default slow tail is the highest 10% of numeric observations; ties at the cutoff can include more rows than the nominal fraction. Results are associations, not causal effects, and binary settings may be confounded by the experiment design. Only numeric measurement rows are analyzed.
