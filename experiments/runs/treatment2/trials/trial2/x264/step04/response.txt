Assumptions and limitations:
- Performance- is treated as higher-is-better, matching the earlier certification behavior.
- Each arriving result contains every binary setting and one finite numeric measurement.
- Configuration results are aggregated by arithmetic running mean; no confidence intervals, stopping rule, or uncertainty-aware ranking is added.
- Ties are resolved lexicographically by the binary configuration tuple.
- The online CLI currently streams rows from a CSV in file order; callers needing live external benchmarks should call OnlineEvaluator.add_result() from their own standard-library integration.
