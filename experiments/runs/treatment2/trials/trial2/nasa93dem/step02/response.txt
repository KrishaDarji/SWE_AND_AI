Assumptions and limitations:
- Effort is estimated from historical effort/KLOC ratios and assumes KLOC is a
  meaningful size predictor.
- The default neighborhood is the seven closest historical KLOC values.
- The reported range is the min/max effort implied by those selected cases,
  not a statistical confidence interval.
- No similarity features beyond KLOC, outlier treatment, validation split, or
  approval policy is implemented.
- Proposed KLOC and historical KLOC must be positive.
