Known assumptions and limitations:
- Budget and capacity are expressed in the same effort units as the dataset.
- Approval requires both checks to pass; otherwise the proposal is held.
- The screening range is based on nearest-project effort/KLOC rates and is not a calibrated probabilistic forecast.
- Proposals are assessed independently; callers must decrement portfolio limits after an approval.
- Only the Python standard library is used.
