Why this is preferable

ExtraTrees was a close alternative: utility MAE 0.01255, utility RMSE 0.02601, and utility R2 0.98070. The selected random forest was marginally better on MAE and tied on utility R2/RMSE, while remaining a conventional, interpretable ensemble for this mixed numeric/categorical search space. The mean baseline was substantially weaker: utility MAE 0.14962 and utility R2 approximately 0.0. Thus the recommendation has strong predictive support and is preferable to a no-signal baseline; its advantage over ExtraTrees is small, so the choice should not be treated as decisive on model family alone.

Uncertainty and limitations

The recommendation is the best observed row in the training partition, not a claim that the configuration will dominate every future workload. The held-out evaluation is one random split rather than repeated cross-validation, and the dataset may reflect a fixed workload or benchmark generation process. The outcomes are bounded and PRED40+ has only eight distinct values, which can make small score differences unstable. The utility weights all three components equally and normalizes MRE by reversal; a platform team with different operational costs should re-rank configurations with its actual weights. Finally, the surrogate predicts benchmark outcomes from hyperparameters; it does not establish causal performance, production latency, memory use, or robustness to distribution shift. Validate this configuration on an independent workload before deployment.
