Approach: load and validate the CSV, enforce a documented quality constraint by requiring all quality-sensitive no_* switches to remain zero, select the feasible row with the lowest measured Performance-, and fit a scikit-learn Ridge model with a held-out score as a ranking sanity check.

Assumptions and limitations: the dataset has no quality metric or threshold, so quality is represented by preserving the listed quality-sensitive features. Performance- is treated as a cost to minimize. This is an empirical selector over the supplied measurements, not a guarantee for unseen encodes.
