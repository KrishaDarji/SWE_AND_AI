Approach: parse the CSV with the standard library, report per-setting ranges and cardinality, report min/mean/max for Performance-, and fit a standardized scikit-learn linear regression to rank setting associations.

Assumptions and limitations: Performance- is treated as the sole quality measure because it is the only non-setting column. Binary settings are represented numerically as 0/1. Coefficients describe linear association in this historical dataset, not causation; correlated settings and constant columns can make coefficient interpretation unstable.
