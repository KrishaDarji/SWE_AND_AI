Approach:
Read the 93 projects, use the process-driver columns as categorical features, one-hot encode them, and fit a seeded RandomForestRegressor to Defects-. For every observed rating, simulate the next more favorable ordinal rating across all projects. Select the one-step candidate with positive predicted risk reduction, minimizing cost first and breaking ties by larger reduction. The measured recommendation is rely: l -> n, with estimated risk reduction of 414.6781 defects-equivalent units.

Known assumptions and limitations:
- Defects- is used as the risk proxy.
- Ratings are treated as ordered categories and adjacent movement has unit cost.
- The direction/order of COCOMO-style ratings is encoded in the source and is an approximation for mixed-scale process attributes.
- Predictions are in-sample model estimates; this small dataset does not establish causal impact.
- No change is recommended if all simulated changes fail to reduce predicted risk.
