Approach:
Read NASA93 with the standard-library csv module, remove identifiers and unavailable outcome columns, preserve numeric features, and encode categorical features with scikit-learn DictVectorizer. Fit a deterministic RandomForestRegressor and estimate Effort- in person-months. The scoring helper uses a deterministic 80/20 holdout.

Measured result:
All 4 tests passed. The timed implementation run returned 133.61 person-months and took 0.66 seconds of real time (the complete captured output is in runtime_output.txt).

Assumptions and limitations:
- Effort- is treated as person-months and Kloc+ is available before approval.
- Defects-, MonthsX, and idX are excluded to avoid using outcomes or identifiers.
- This is a small historical dataset, so estimates can be unstable for proposals unlike the training data.
- The model gives a point estimate only; it does not provide uncertainty or an approval threshold.
