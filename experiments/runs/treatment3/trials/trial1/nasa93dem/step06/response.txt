Approach:
Load the 93 historical projects, treat Kloc+ and Effort- as numeric fields and
the remaining planning attributes as categorical fields, vectorize them with
scikit-learn DictVectorizer, and fit a seeded RandomForestRegressor. Five-fold
cross-validation estimates absolute error. The final report uses the fitted
estimate plus mean error and fold-to-fold error spread to form a planning range,
then states the evidence, recommendation, and risks for a skeptical sponsor.

Known assumptions and limitations:
- MonthsX is the duration target and idX is excluded as an identifier.
- The final historical row is used only as a simple independent check in the
  report; it is not a new project forecast.
- The range is a pragmatic error-based planning interval, not a calibrated
  statistical confidence or prediction interval.
- The dataset is small and historical; scope growth, staffing, technology,
  changing processes, and distribution shift may make the estimate unreliable.
- The implementation expects the supplied CSV schema and numeric Kloc+/Effort-
  values.
