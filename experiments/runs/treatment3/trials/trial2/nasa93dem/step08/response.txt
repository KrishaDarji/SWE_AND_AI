Assumptions and limitations:
- Defects- is used as the risk proxy; project risk could instead include effort, schedule, or a business-specific loss function.
- Cost is a relative ordinal change cost, not a monetary implementation estimate.
- The random forest is a predictive association model. Its recommendation is not evidence that changing acap causes fewer defects; confounding, selection effects, historical practice, and model extrapolation remain possible.
- The baseline is synthetic (modes/means), so it may not correspond to a real project. The large cross-validated MAE shows substantial uncertainty, and the estimate should be validated with domain review or a controlled intervention before adoption.
- The dataset is small, historical, and observational; this analysis does not establish causal effects or guarantee the estimated reduction.
