The evaluator reads the CSV through DictReader and processes one row at a time. It validates the required identifier and three planning outcomes (effort, defects, months), rejects missing, nonnumeric, nonfinite, or negative values, and keeps a reproducible running state. The score is a weighted cost with equal weights after fixed planning scales: effort/1000 + defects/10000 + months/100. Lower scores win; proposal ID breaks exact score ties lexicographically.

Assumptions and limitations: the three selected outcomes are minimized and treated as equally important after fixed scales; the scales are planning conventions rather than learned values. The flow does not infer missing values, use batch statistics, or claim causal/project success validity. Proposal IDs are compared as strings for deterministic tie handling.
