The loader validates the header, non-empty rows, binary settings, numeric performance, and required quality columns. The quality constraint is a documented policy: no disabling of CABAC, deblocking, MB-tree, mixed references, weighted prediction, or 9-reference mode (`0` for each `no_*`/`ref_9` quality feature). Among the 24 feasible rows, the objective minimizes the measured `Performance-` value.

Performance value: 254.587 (lower is better).

If no row satisfies the policy, `choose` raises a clear `LookupError` and the tests cover that path. The dataset has no independent visual-quality metric, so the quality result is policy compliance, not measured perceptual quality. The policy is conservative and may exclude faster configurations; the selected result is empirical for this dataset and does not establish causal effects or generalization to other workloads.
