Conditions: The advantage is demonstrated for this x264 table, split, seed, model, and 24-candidate budget. It should not be generalized to other seeds, budgets, distributions, or encoding workloads without repeated evaluation.

Uncertainty and limitations: This is one fixed split and one random baseline draw, so no confidence interval or statistical significance claim is made. The exhaustive dataset makes an oracle comparison possible, but it does not measure real encoding wall-clock cost, model-training overhead, or measurement noise. Random-forest predictions can be sensitive to sparse combinations and the chosen hyperparameters. The baseline is intentionally simple and may be weaker than an optimized search method. Possible bias includes favorable split structure, duplicate-free but potentially correlated configurations, target drift, and selection bias from judging on a single held-out partition. Repeated seeded splits, multiple baseline replicates, confidence intervals, and real execution-cost measurements would strengthen the conclusion.
