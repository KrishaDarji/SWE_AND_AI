Implemented an independent blame analysis in source/blame_analysis.py. It validates the exact schema and complete rows, treats MRE- as the model-error target, and defines high error as MRE- at or above the empirical 75th percentile. A stratified holdout logistic regression (standardized numeric fields and one-hot criterion) predicts the binary high-error label. Permutation importance on holdout ROC AUC ranks the hyperparameters; Pearson association and group means provide a secondary, interpretable check.

Interpretation: permutation importance is predictive association, not causal effect. The ranking can be affected by correlated hyperparameters, the arbitrary quartile threshold, sampling variation, model specification, and the fact that this is observational/generated HPO data. ACC+ and PRED40+ may be downstream performance measures rather than causes; treating them as predictors can create leakage when the intended use is prospective tuning. The single split and ten permutation repeats do not provide formal confidence intervals. Results should be confirmed with repeated or nested validation, prespecified causal contrasts or randomized tuning interventions, and checks for duplicate configurations and data-generation effects.
