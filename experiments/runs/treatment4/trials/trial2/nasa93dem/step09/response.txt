Implemented Step 9 spot screening in `source/nasa93_summary.py`, preserving all Step 8 interfaces and tests.

The new `spot_inconsistencies` function screens Kloc+, Effort-, and Defects- with a robust univariate rule: nonnegative outcomes are transformed with `log1p`; the reference is the median transformed value; the scale is `1.4826 * MAD`; and a record is flagged when the absolute modified score is at least 3.5. Negative values are explicitly flagged as invalid, while missing, non-finite, and non-numeric values are excluded from the reference calculation. Constant and zero-scale samples are handled deterministically. Reference statistics include the assessed record.

The NASA93 demonstration completed with 0 flags. The complete suite passed: 31 tests. Demonstration timing from `/usr/bin/time -p`: real 0.80 seconds.

Flags are investigation candidates, not confirmed errors. Large projects can legitimately have large effort or defect counts. This univariate screen does not establish inconsistency conditional on project scope, anomaly-detection accuracy, or prediction uncertainty.

Known assumptions and limitations: `idX` is used as the identifier when present, otherwise the zero-based CSV row index is used; outcomes are interpreted as numeric nonnegative quantities; the assessed record is included in its field's reference statistics; the method is marginal and univariate; and no causal explanation, units, labels, costs, or prediction uncertainty is inferred.
