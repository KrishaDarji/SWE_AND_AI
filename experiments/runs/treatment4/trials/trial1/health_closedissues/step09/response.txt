Method
The new reusable screen_rows(rows, threshold=3.5) and screen(dataset, threshold=3.5) functions perform descriptive two-sided marginal robust screening. Configuration context fields: N_estimators, criterion, Min_sample_leaves, Min_impurity_decrease, Max_depth. Screened objectives: MRE- (minimize), ACC+ (maximize), PRED40+ (maximize). No objective is used to predict another objective; there are no predictions or residuals.

Reuse: Step 1 load_rows/schema and field roles, Step 7 normalize_records, and its existing Step 2 configuration and Step 4 outcome normalization machinery. Values normalize to finite floats, criterion strings are trimmed, blanks/omitted values become missing. No objective imputation or model fitting occurs. Configuration fields supply observed context and exact normalized duplicate-configuration grouping only; they do not enter the anomaly score.

For each objective with at least five observations, compute the median and median absolute deviation (MAD). Scale is 1.4826*MAD. If MAD is zero, use IQR/1.349; if IQR is also zero, use the observed range; a constant column receives zero scores. Quartiles interpolate at (n-1)*p. An objective score is abs(observed-median)/scale. Missing values and objectives with fewer than five observed records receive null scores. The record score is the maximum available objective score (null if all unavailable). This is a descriptive distance from the observed distribution, not an unseen prediction error or optimization-quality score.

Flag a record only when its score is strictly greater than 3.5, equivalent to OR across scored objectives. The cutoff is heuristic, with no probability, estimated error-rate, or multiple-testing guarantee. It imposes no quota. Larger scores mean more unusual in either tail; objective directions only label better/worse relative to the median. Ties sort by ascending one-based CSV data-row identity, and unscored rows sort last. All records are retained in anomalies.json; --display-limit affects console output only. No randomness is involved.

Scores describe marginal rarity; they cannot establish conditional unexpectedness, metric correctness, causes, or future performance. MAD/IQR fallbacks are conservative for concentrated/discrete distributions; the range fallback can suppress flags. Selective missingness, differing objective subsets and small samples limit coverage. Missing configurations compare equal for context only. Exact normalized equality defines ties; float rounding is possible and arithmetic overflow is rejected.
