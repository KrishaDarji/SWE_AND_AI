Assumptions and limitations:
This comparison uses recorded outcomes retrospectively. The exhaustive selector sees and ranks all feasible objectives while random selection does not optimize values; this is an information and search-effort advantage. Lexicographic wins are expected by construction under the selector's own preference rule. The results establish neither learning effectiveness, sample efficiency, prospective improvement nor statistical superiority. Seeds remain within Trial 1 and are not independent LLM trials. CSV row order is record identity, not chronology. Duplicates remain separate rows. Missing data are excluded without imputation. Float comparisons are exact after normalization, with finite differences required. Metric formulas, units, practical acceptance thresholds and representativeness are unspecified. No measurement repeatability or deployment guarantee is established.
