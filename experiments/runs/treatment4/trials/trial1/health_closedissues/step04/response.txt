A new FlowEvaluator.update(configuration, outcomes) validates separate inputs

The evaluator retains fixed-size state, without rescanning past observations.

Arrival and statistical conventions
Original CSV record order simulates arrivals; it is not verified chronological
order. Each update uses only that arrival and existing accumulators. Empty,
whitespace-only, omitted, or None outcomes are missing, excluded separately
from each objective's moments and best selection, and counted without imputation.
Configuration missingness does not itself exclude an observed objective.
Numeric strings are normalized to finite floats; invalid and misplaced keys,
booleans, non-finite numbers, and overflowing moment arithmetic are rejected
without changing state. MRE- is minimized; ACC+ and PRED40+ are maximized.
Mean is null for zero observations; sample variance and standard deviation
use denominator n-1 and are null for fewer than two observations.
Exact normalized float ties retain the earliest arrival; all arrivals attaining
the current best count toward best_tie_count. Repeated configurations count as
separate arrivals. No combined objective score is computed.

Assumptions and limitations
These are descriptive recorded outcomes. Row order does not establish time,
randomization or independence. Missingness may be selective; objectives may
use different observed subsets. No metric formula, physical unit, compute cost,
parameter domain, theoretical metric range, or uncertainty guarantee is inferred.
Float rounding affects near ties and moments; extreme overflowing inputs are
rejected. Best observed does not establish future performance or optimality.
No predictions, repeated significance tests, stopping guarantees, or streaming
statistical-significance claims are added. The preserved Step 3 comparison runs
separately as a regression demo with its existing assumptions and limitations.
Evaluator state is constant-size; retaining/exporting the complete history uses
linear memory. Earlier steps' original limitations remain applicable.

All requested outputs, regression artifacts, and measurement files are confined
to this Step 4 directory. Dataset and Step 3 inputs were not modified.
