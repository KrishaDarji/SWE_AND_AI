Scope and preservation
Only Step 8 output files were written. No dataset or Step 7 file was modified.
The seven Step 7 source modules and seven test modules are copied byte-for-byte.
The original test_guess.py expects example_configuration.json beside source/;
that fixture was not among the allowed previous inputs. It was newly generated
from the supplied Health CSV data row 1, without inspecting any previous fixture.
The initial full suite failed on that missing fixture (79/80 passed). The failure
and initial measurements are retained as initial_test_output.txt,
initial_measurement_output.txt, initial_metrics.csv and initial_runtime_output.txt.
The fixture was then added and the full suite and all demos rerun.
measurement_attempts.jsonl retains subprocess commands, exit codes and timings.
Unexpected failed subprocess attempts count once, without counting the wrapper
exit that reports the same failure again. Expected invalid-input tests and
expected absent-directory/search probes are excluded from error_count.
The initial standalone Step 8 suite passed 11/11; see step08_initial_tests.txt.

Interpretation
Cost is the number of unequal normalized settings, regardless of numeric
magnitude, without categorical ordering or measured operational costs.
Rows are observations; duplicates remain separate and may yield cost zero.
Missing settings or MRE exclude a candidate, with overlapping reason counts.
Missing ACC or PRED40 does not exclude a candidate and remains explicit null.
The baseline requires all settings and observed MRE, but not secondary metrics.
Finite numeric floats and trimmed categorical strings reuse previous validation;
there is no newly inferred estimator parameter domain or metric formula.
Strict improvement and tie equality use exact normalized floating comparisons.
The baseline and all other rows are searched without an estimator cap.
No independent validation, intervention, prediction, or repeatability claim is made.
Preserved earlier demos retain their original behavior, including their own
assumptions and limitations; Step 8 does not use their predictions or inference.
All test temporary directories in the full run were kept inside Step 8.
