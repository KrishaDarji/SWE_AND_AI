Evaluation:
10,000 records, 10,000 distinct normalized configuration groups; 8,000 training
and 2,000 held-out groups/rows. Split uses GroupShuffleSplit, test_size=0.2,
random_state=42, with first-seen group IDs from predictor tuples only. Identical
normalized configurations stay together even when their objectives differ.
The ceiling of 20% of distinct groups is held out. Row weights are equal;
repeated configurations would retain all of their observations in one split.
The actual data have no repeated configuration groups. IDs and all group
configuration values are recorded in evaluation.json, along with sample sizes
and every scored held-out prediction.

Each target has its own fixed RandomForestRegressor: 120 trees, max_depth=12,
min_samples_leaf=2, max_features=1.0, bootstrap=True, squared_error criterion,
random_state=42, n_jobs=1. Settings were fixed without holdout tuning. This
forest models configuration-to-metric behavior; the input N_estimators and
criterion fields are configuration observations, not surrogate hyperparameters.
Numeric medians and categorical mode/one-hot preprocessing are learned only
from labeled training rows for the particular target. No objective enters X.

Assumptions and limitations:
- Targets are recorded CSV metrics; no metric formula, unit, cost, or causal meaning is inferred.
- A single seed-42 group holdout estimates performance on unseen configurations from this dataset only.
- No tuning uses held-out targets; model settings are fixed in advance.
- Unseen categories encode as all zero; unseen numeric values may extrapolate poorly.
- Missing numeric inputs use training medians (zero for entirely missing columns); missing criterion uses the training mode (zero category if entirely missing).
- Missing target labels are excluded separately per target; neither targets nor PRED40+ enter predictors.
- No theoretical metric ranges are assumed or imposed. Forest predictions are within the training target range; this does not guarantee validity on unseen configurations.
- Predictions are point estimates with no uncertainty or accuracy guarantee; evaluation MAE is in each recorded target scale.
- Numeric inputs follow Step 1 finite-float validation; no undocumented parameter-domain or integer restrictions are added.
- Row IDs are one-based data-record positions, not persistent identifiers; header is CSV line 1.
- Entirely missing categorical training columns use SimpleImputer's zero
  fallback. No ordinal mapping is invented for observed criterion labels.
- Missing held-out targets are omitted from scoring, independently per target;
  the split records all rows. If either partition lacks labels for a target,
  evaluation fails explicitly. At least two distinct groups are required.
- Observed training ranges are recorded per target but are not assumed to be
  theoretical metric bounds. Predictions are not clipped to a presumed range.
- Seed reproducibility applies to the recorded data order and dependencies;
  reordered input may receive a different deterministic group assignment.
