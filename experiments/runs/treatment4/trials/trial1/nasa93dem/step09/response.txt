Method: for Kloc+, Effort-, Defects-, transform observed values with log1p, compute each field's historical median and median absolute deviation, and divide each absolute deviation by max(1.4826*MAD, log(2)). A project's score is the maximum available field score. Higher scores are more unusual. Flag only score strictly greater than 3.5; equal scores are not flagged. The floor is a documented practical convention to avoid zero/near-zero scales, not a learned or calibrated threshold. This transparent robust method addresses skewed outcomes without arbitrary categorical ordering; no additional sklearn estimator is needed for this descriptive calculation. The preserved sklearn model continues to use seed 42.

Outcome fields are retrospective screening inputs, not proposal-time inputs. Categorical candidate proposal fields appear only as descriptive characteristics with support counts; their availability at proposal time is assumed and must be checked. Identifiers, all X-suffixed metadata and unknown fields do not enter scores. No effort predictions or prediction residuals are used. Each historical record contributes to the descriptive reference; these are not unseen prediction errors.

Missing outcomes are not imputed. Reference summaries omit missing values separately by field. Fewer than three observed values makes that field unscorable; a record with no usable observed outcomes receives a null score, no flag and an explicit unscored status. Invalid negative/nonfinite outcomes are rejected. No randomness is used by screening. Ties sort by one-based CSV data-row number, excluding the header; identifiers never establish row identity.
