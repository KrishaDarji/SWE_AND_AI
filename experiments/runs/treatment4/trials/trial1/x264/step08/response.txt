Implementation and reuse
Complete updated application code is in source/: remember.py, guess.py,
certify.py, blame.py, choose.py, flow.py, justify.py, and the new fix.py.
The seven previous modules and their seven test files are byte-identical to
allowed Step 7 inputs. They preserve Steps 1–7 behavior. New Step 8 tests are
in tests/test_fix.py. No dataset or previous-step files were modified.

fix.recommend_repair(data, baseline_row) reuses remember.Measurements and
blame._validate; its CLI reuses remember.load_measurements. It scans every
recorded row with strictly lower Performance- than the baseline observation,
minimizing (number of differing setting columns, observed Performance-, CSV
row number). It excludes the objective from distance, uses exact numeric
comparisons, and returns every changed column in original column order.
Rows are numbered from 1 after the CSV header. The dataset path and baseline
row are CLI arguments. No scikit-learn estimator is appropriate for this
exhaustive observed-row task; the existing scikit-learn prediction and search
modules remain available and were tested with installed scikit-learn 1.6.0.
Time complexity is O(recorded rows * setting columns).

Assumptions and limitations
This is an offline full-data search over recorded observations, not budgeted
search or an unseen-configuration evaluation. All non-objective columns are
encoded settings, parsed as finite numeric floats by the existing loader.
Encoded-column distance is a proxy for change size, not measured operational
cost; redundant or one-hot columns may represent one underlying choice.
Only complete recorded configurations are returned. Duplicate configurations
are not averaged: a lower recorded observation for identical settings can win
at zero change cost, without a setting repair. No-improvement results explicitly
return status=no_improving_candidate, null recommendation/cost/reduction, and
an empty change list. Equal objectives never qualify as improvement.
A lower historical observation does not establish causal, repeatable, or
statistically certified improvement. Selection can be optimistic and historical
conditions may differ. No quality measurements are invented and quality
preservation is not established. Exact float equality governs ties; numeric
rounding can affect comparisons. Unsupported nonfinite reductions are rejected.
