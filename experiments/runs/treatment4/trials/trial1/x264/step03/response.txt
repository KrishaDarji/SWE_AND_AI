Question and limitation
No separate output-quality measure exists in the actual dataset. Performance- is minimized,
and physical units are unspecified. Therefore a quality improvement cannot be certified.
The demonstration evaluates a Performance- improvement association only.

Observed schema
1152 rows; 16 numeric binary encoder settings, followed by Performance-:
no_asm, no_8x8dct, no_cabac, no_deblock, no_fast_pskip, no_mbtree,
no_mixed_refs, no_weightb, rc_lookahead, rc_lookahead_20, rc_lookahead_40,
rc_lookahead_60, ref, ref_1, ref_5, ref_9, Performance-.
no_asm, rc_lookahead and ref are constant 1. All 1152 configurations are unique;
there are no repeated-configuration observations. Input paths were verified first.

Group-selection rule (specified before interpreting the results)
Baseline: every observed row with no_8x8dct=0.
Candidate: every observed row with no_8x8dct=1.
No objective ranking, favorable-value filtering, predictions or group search is used.
Encoded flag semantics beyond the recorded values are not inferred.

Procedure
Two-sided permutation test of absolute difference in sample means, alpha=0.05.
Exact enumeration is used if C(n+m,n)<=50000. Otherwise 19999 random label
permutations are generated with numpy default_rng seed 42031. Monte Carlo
p=(extreme+1)/(19999+1), avoiding a zero reported p-value. Ties are counted
as extreme, with a numerical tolerance of 1e-12 on the scaled statistic.
This distribution-free test is appropriate under independent observations and
exchangeable labels under the null of identical distributions, rather than relying
on a normality model. It is not a heteroskedasticity-robust test of equal means alone.
Historical configuration data do not establish these inferential assumptions;
the certification flag is explicitly conditional on them.

Practical criterion: observed improvement must be positive and at least 1.0
objective unit. This is a declared illustrative threshold, not a domain-validated
benefit. Users may supply a different threshold. The combined certification flag
requires p<=0.05 and this observed-effect criterion. It does not certify that a
population effect exceeds 1.0: no effect confidence bound is calculated.

Interpretation and assumptions
The comparison is an association among different encoder configurations.
Other settings may differ between groups. No random assignment or causal effect
is established. Configurations are not demonstrated independent draws from a
population, and hidden dependence or unequal distributions can invalidate the
permutation interpretation. This comparison does not establish repeatability of
an individual configuration, especially since none has repeated measurements.
No model predictions enter certification as independent observed measurements.
One specified comparison was run; there was no multiple-comparison search.
A nonsignificant result means no detected difference, not proof of equivalence.
Small samples are allowed down to two per group but can have little power and
coarse exact p-values. Inputs must be finite real observations; floating-point
arithmetic imposes numeric-range and precision limits. No resampling procedure
can repair a lack of measurement independence or demonstrate output quality here.

Reuse and additions
remember.py, guess.py and both previous test modules are byte-identical copies
of the allowed Step 2 implementation/tests. Descriptive summaries, grouped
holdout, prediction input checks, training-only fitting and the scikit-learn
baseline/model are preserved. certify.py adds a reusable two-sample API, validation,
exact/seeded statistical evidence, practical threshold, clear conclusions and
an argument-driven dataset CLI producing certification.json.
Ten new tests cover exact p=2/70, reversal and maximize direction, identical
samples, small samples, unequal group sizes and ties (manual p=4/10), practical
gating, invalid data/options, Monte Carlo reproducibility and plus-one correction,
objective-independent group selection, real-data means, CLI output and overwrite protection.
