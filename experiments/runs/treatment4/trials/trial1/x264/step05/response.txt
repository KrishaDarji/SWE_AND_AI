Actual schema
The dataset has 1152 rows, 16 settings and one registered objective,
Performance-. All setting values observed are binary. The three constant
settings are no_asm=1, rc_lookahead=1 and ref=1. Performance- is minimized;
physical units are unspecified. All settings exclude the objective.

Method
For each nonconstant setting, group all rows by exact observed value, then
rank by categorical eta squared:
  sum_v n_v * (mean_y_v - mean_y)^2 / sum_i (y_i - mean_y)^2.
The score is the observed fraction of total squared target variation between
that setting's group means. It lies in [0,1]. Target scaling is recorded in
blame.json and leaves the ratio unchanged. Zero target variance gives score 0.
Constants have no rank and a null score. Exact score ties share competition
rank (1,1,3); tied display order is alphabetical. No tolerance is applied.
Direction is reported separately using group means, differences from the overall
mean, highest/lowest mean values, and sample counts. All mean extrema are
retained on ties. The JSON includes count, fraction, min, quartiles, median,
max, mean and population standard deviation for each value.

No model is fitted for Step 5. No seed, training partition, holdout evaluation,
p-value or significance claim is applicable. Rankings are exploratory
observational descriptions. This is not fitted-model feature importance;
importance or separation scores alone do not give direction. Every variable
pair has a recorded Pearson correlation and an exact check for equivalence
of observed value partitions (including complementary or relabeled values).

Assumptions and limitations
- Associations are observational, marginal, exploratory and not causal.
  Confounding, interactions, dependence and sampling choices remain possible.
- Correlated/redundant encoded choices can share credit or constrain valid
  combinations. Scores must not be added; pair correlations do not resolve
  confounding, and zero Pearson correlation does not imply independence.
- Exact observed numeric values are categories. High-cardinality settings and
  singleton groups can inflate the score. Binary dataset findings do not
  guarantee generalization to new workloads, hardware or unseen settings.
- Every row is weighted equally; duplicates remain. Counts are not independent
  replicate counts, uncertainty estimates or evidence of repeatability.
- Constants are uninformative for comparison, not proven to have no effect.
  A zero mean-separation score does not rule out interactions or variance shifts.
- No multiple significance testing is performed. No confidence intervals or
  significance guarantees are provided. Small or near-tied scores can be unstable.
- Units and separate output quality are unavailable. No seconds, quality
  preservation or practical improvement threshold is inferred.
- Finite numeric inputs are required. Floating-point arithmetic has range and
  rounding limits; encoder flag/combination validity is not established.
