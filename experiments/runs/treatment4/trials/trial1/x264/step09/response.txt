The actual dataset has 1152 rows and 1152 distinct
configurations, 16 numeric encoder settings and one minimized outcome,
Performance-, whose physical units are unspecified. All 16 settings are
retained in each reported configuration. The distance fields, in CSV order,
are no_8x8dct, no_cabac, no_deblock, no_fast_pskip, no_mbtree, no_mixed_refs,
no_weightb, rc_lookahead_20, rc_lookahead_40, rc_lookahead_60, ref_1, ref_5,
ref_9. no_asm, rc_lookahead and ref are constant and excluded from distance.
Performance- never enters setting distance.

Method: deterministic leave-configuration-out local median discrepancy. Each
variable setting is range-normalized to [0,1] over distinct configurations.
Distance is mean Manhattan distance over those fields. For each configuration,
exclude every row of its exact setting tuple; take the 10 nearest distinct
configurations, including all distance ties within absolute tolerance 1e-12.
For smaller inputs, k is capped at distinct configurations minus one. At least
three distinct configurations are required; smaller inputs raise ValueError.
Each neighboring configuration contributes its median Performance- with equal
weight. The local reference is the median of those configuration medians.
This is an outcome reference from other configurations, not a training residual.
There is no fitted prediction model.

The score is abs(observed Performance- minus local reference), with larger
scores indicating greater discrepancy in either direction. Internally targets
are divided by max(1,max(abs(target))) for numerical protection; scores and
references are restored to original unspecified units. Setting extrema and
the internal divisor are saved in anomalies.json.

Let m=median(all row scores), MAD=median(abs(score-m)), and R=max(outcomes)-
min(outcomes). Flag strictly when score > m + 3*max(1.4826*MAD,0.01*R).
Equality does not flag. Constant outcomes yield zero scores, threshold zero,
and no flags when sufficient distinct configurations exist. The range floor
handles zero MAD without selecting a fixed number of rows. This threshold
is a descriptive screening heuristic, not an estimated error rate or p-value.
It uses all residuals, so it is not independent predictive validation.

The JSON ranks ALL 1152 records, highest score first; exact score ties
share competition rank and display in ascending 1-based CSV data-row order
(header excluded). Every record has its score, threshold, flag, full observed
configuration and Performance-, local reference, signed discrepancy, neighbor
row identities, excluded own-configuration identities, distances, neighbor
outcome range and MAD, and explanation. Every flagged record has suggested
client checks. --top defaults to 10 and limits text display only; it does not
change the threshold, ranked JSON, or flagged count.

Assumptions and limitations: equal range-normalized numeric setting weights
may poorly reflect encoding semantics, especially with redundant indicators.
Large setting interactions or sparse neighborhoods can flag valid results;
clustered errors may escape detection. Configuration medians reduce duplicate

Historical configurations are not controlled replicates. No anomaly labels,
precision/recall, confidence bounds, causal effects, calibrated error rates
or independent predictive-accuracy claims are available. Floating-point
resolution and quadratic distance storage limit extreme/large inputs.
