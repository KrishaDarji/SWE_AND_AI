Implemented Step 5 blame analysis and preserved all Step 4 interfaces and regression tests.

Method: MRE- is the only explanatory outcome; ACC+ and PRED40+ are excluded. Numeric settings use squared Pearson correlation (r^2), and criterion uses eta-squared. Both are marginal proportions of observed MRE- variation, so the same score is used for ranking. Lower MRE- is better. Missing/non-finite values are excluded for numeric pairs and represented as a categorical missing level for criterion. Constants and insufficient samples receive a non-informative score; ties are deterministic by setting name. No causal claim is made, and correlated settings may share explanatory information.
