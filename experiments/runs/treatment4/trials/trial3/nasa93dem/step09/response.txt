Implemented Step 9 spot screening and preserved the complete Step 8 source and tests.

The new `source/nasa93_spot.py` uses a marginal robust univariate screen: it applies
log1p to nonnegative Kloc+, Effort-, and Defects-, centers each outcome at its median,
and scores by absolute deviation divided by 1.4826 times MAD. The threshold is 3.5,
and reference statistics include the assessed record. Missing, non-finite, and negative
values are excluded with explicit reasons while original idX identifiers are retained.
Zero robust scale produces a null score and flags only non-center values. JSON output
uses null rather than NaN or Infinity. Flags are investigation candidates, not confirmed
errors; large projects may legitimately have large effort or defect counts. This is a
marginal screen and makes no scope-adjusted, accuracy, causal, or prediction-uncertainty
claim.

Validation: 42 tests passed, including all copied Step 8 regression tests and Step 9
synthetic tests. The NASA93 demonstration completed successfully and found 0 flagged
records at threshold 3.5. Its measured `/usr/bin/time -p` real time was 0.66 seconds.

Run from the Step 9 directory:

    python3 -m pytest -q tests
    python3 source/nasa93_spot.py "/Users/vivek/D Drive/vs code/SWEAI_Project/SWE_AND_AI/moot/optimize/process/nasa93dem.csv"

Known limitations: this is a marginal, univariate candidate screen; it does not adjust
for project scope, model interactions, labels, or causal mechanisms. Including the
assessed record in its reference statistics can moderate its own score. MAD can be zero
for tied data, in which case non-center values are flagged without a finite score.
