Implemented Treatment 4, Trial 3, Dataset 1, Step 5 in source/summarize_x264.py.

The new blame() API and --blame CLI rank encoder settings by one consistent marginal eta-squared score: between-setting outcome variation divided by total outcome variation. Lower Performance- is better. Missing/non-finite settings become an explicit group; invalid targets are excluded; constants, insufficient groups, zero outcome variance, and ties are handled explicitly. Each result includes group counts, means, minima, maxima, fastest/slowest groups, and status. Correlation, mutual exclusivity, observational limits, and non-causal interpretation are documented in the result.
